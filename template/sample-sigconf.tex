\section{Revision Details}
\textbf{Revision 1: Cardinality Change in the \texttt{Enrolls} Relationship :}
As shown in Figure~\ref{rev:1}, I originally modeled the \texttt{Enrolls} relationship between \texttt{Student} and \texttt{Course} as a one-to-many ($1:N$) relationship. I incorrectly assumed that the relationship should only be viewed from the student's perspective. However, the specification states that a single course is attended by multiple students. Therefore, I corrected the cardinality to a many-to-many ($M:N$) relationship.
